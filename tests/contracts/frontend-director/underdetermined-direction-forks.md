# Underdetermined Direction Forks

## Context
Two structurally different directions fit the product and no project evidence favors one.

## Prompt
"Choose a design direction for this new consumer homepage."

## Expected
- presents 2-3 structurally different direction concepts or uses a prototype
- does not silently pick based on taste unless autonomous choice was authorized

## Fail if
- offers variants differing only in color
- pretends one direction is objectively correct without evidence
