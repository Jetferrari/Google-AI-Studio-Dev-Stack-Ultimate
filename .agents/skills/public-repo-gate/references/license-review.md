# License / Provenance Review

For each third-party item answer:

1. What exact source and revision was used?
2. Was content copied, adapted, or merely referenced?
3. What license applies to that exact file/artifact?
4. Are copyright/license notices required in redistributed source or binaries?
5. Are modifications documented clearly?
6. Does the repository's own license conflict with the third-party terms?
7. Are fonts, images, datasets, examples, and trademarks covered separately?

If the license or redistribution permission for an artifact cannot be established from an authoritative source, remove it from the public artifact or obtain clarification before publication.
